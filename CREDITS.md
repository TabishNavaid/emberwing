Only these small crops from licensed packs ship with the game, in `public/sprites/`. The full packs aren't in the repo: their licenses allow using them in projects (commercial or not, modified or not) but not resharing or reselling the packs themselves.

## Music

The game points guests to *From the Motion Picture How to Train Your Dragon* by John Powell (arr. O'Loughlin), which the Walla Walla Symphony Youth Orchestra plays in Act II of Symphonicon. No recording or melody from that score is in the game. Our tune and all the sound effects were written for this project and are synthesized live with Web Audio.

## Everything else

The dragons, critters, scenery, pixel font and music were all made for this project, in code. Nothing from the How to Train Your Dragon films is used: no characters, names, places, logos, dragon designs or music.
